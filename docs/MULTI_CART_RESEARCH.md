# Multiple carts at the clothing rental counter

## What the sources show

- Shopify POS autosaves a cart after staff add an item or customer, lets staff reopen it from Recent carts, and removes it after successful checkout. Its default cart name comes from the customer or products, and its recent carts leave inventory available. [Shopify POS recent carts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/manage-recent-carts)
- Square gives staff an explicit Save ticket action and lets them reopen the ticket later. A name identifies the ticket and staff can add an optional note. [Square Open Tickets](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5337-use-open-tickets-with-square)
- Booqable keeps rental dates and items on a draft, then blocks availability for those dates when staff reserve the order. Its mobile POS also shows availability for the selected rental dates. [Booqable order workflow](https://help.booqable.com/en/articles/3845244-the-booqable-order-workflow), [Booqable mobile POS](https://help.booqable.com/en/articles/6579311-how-to-create-and-edit-orders-in-the-booqable-mobile-point-of-sale)
- Baymard’s e-commerce usability research found that the number of fields people must consider matters more than the number of steps and that people overlook poorly labeled guest options. The field and labeling findings can inform a staffed checkout. They compare consumer e-commerce flows. [Checkout form fields](https://baymard.com/research-articles/checkout-flow-average-form-fields), [Guest checkout visibility](https://baymard.com/research-articles/make-guest-checkout-prominent)

## Recommendation for Overbooked

- Keep one active cart with a visible identifier wherever staff add items. Put **New cart** and **Open carts** beside it. Starting or resuming another cart automatically saves the current one if staff have entered anything meaningful. An empty cart disappears when staff switch away. Give discard its own labeled action. Automatic saving removes a step during interruptions, while the visible active cart label helps prevent adding a scan to the wrong cart. [Shopify POS recent carts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/manage-recent-carts), [Square Open Tickets](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5337-use-open-tickets-with-square)
- Show customer name or a stable guest cart label, pickup and return dates, item count, and last update in a most-recent-first list. An item preview helps staff tell similar guest carts apart. Let them give a guest cart an optional fitting cue when needed. On the checkout page, show the active customer or **Guest cart**, rental dates and items, payment, and a booking summary with one confirmation action. Staff can build a guest cart before selecting a customer. The current booking contract requires an existing customer or a new customer with a name and phone at confirmation. [Shopify POS recent carts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/manage-recent-carts), [Square Open Tickets](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5337-use-open-tickets-with-square), [Baymard checkout form fields](https://baymard.com/research-articles/checkout-flow-average-form-fields), [Booking submission](../src/components/shop/useBookingForm.ts), [Customer validation](../src/components/shop/CustomerForm.tsx)
- Treat carts as drafts. Show that inventory remains available until booking. Refresh availability on resume when dates are present, and rely on booking confirmation to enforce availability. Mark the affected items if a piece is no longer available and keep the rest of the cart intact. Remove only the successfully booked cart from the open list. [Booqable order workflow](https://help.booqable.com/en/articles/3845244-the-booqable-order-workflow), [Shopify POS recent carts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/manage-recent-carts), [Rental booking function](../supabase/migrations/20261101000012_cleaning_days_per_booking.sql)
- Keep checkout inputs tied to the cart identifier so resuming restores the correct customer, dates, branch, and lines. The current cart is scoped to a staff member and shop on a device, and the checkout has its own session draft. Extending the current scope keeps the first version simpler. Shared carts would enable counter handoffs and need a rule for simultaneous edits. Shopify POS makes the other editor visible when a cart is already open. [Current cart provider](../src/modules/clothing/cart.tsx), [Booking draft](../src/components/shop/useBookingForm.ts), [Shopify POS recent carts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/manage-recent-carts)

## Implemented scope

The switcher lists carts newest first by creation. Each entry shows the customer name when available, a stable cart number, quantity, branch, and dates when set. A new or resumed cart saves the current one automatically. Staff can discard any cart from checkout, including an empty one. Items are saved per staff member and shop in the browser. Customer and payment fields stay in the checkout tab, and sign-out clears them. Completing a booking removes only its cart.
