# Overbooked: sales sheet

Rental shop software that stops double bookings, tracks deposits and shows the shop's money. Made by Zypher.

> **Before you give this sheet out (for Zypher):** (1) the live system must be running on paid plans with backups switched on; (2) the demo shop must be ready; (3) the commission amounts, the piece limits and the initial costs on these pages must be confirmed in writing; (4) check that BookieBuddy's prices in the comparison are still current (they come from their own price poster). This sheet leaves out costs and weak points on purpose.

## 1. What Overbooked does, in a shop owner's words

**Bookings and stock**
- **No more double bookings.** If a dress is booked or still out, the system will not let it be booked again. It also keeps the dress back for cleaning days.
- **Every piece is tracked.** Each dress, saree or suit is one piece with its own code. You always know where it is.
- **Scan a tag, find the dress.** Each piece gets a QR tag. Scan it to find it, hand it over and take it back.
- **Several branches.** See free and rented pieces in each branch. Staff see only their own branch.
- **A calendar** that shows who has which piece and when.
- **Sets, measurements and alterations** (switched on for the shops that want them).
- **Appointments** for fittings, trials and deliveries.

**Money**
- **Deposits are clear.** What was paid, what is owed, what deposit is held, what was given back. Nothing is given back by mistake while a piece is out.
- **Late and lost charges** are suggested for you. A lost piece is charged at its replacement value.
- **A daily cash count** by cash, UPI, card and bank, so the drawer matches.
- **Expenses and profit.** Rent, salaries, dry-cleaning and tailoring, and what is left.
- **Receipts and GST tax invoices.** A plain receipt for shops with no GST number. A numbered tax invoice for shops with one.

**People and safety**
- **Staff see only what they should.** Counter staff do not see the money pages.
- **Every correction is recorded** with a reason. Nothing disappears.
- **Sign-in is protected,** and the shop's data is kept apart from every other shop's.

**Customers and messages**
- **Customer records** with ID, history, and a block list.
- **WhatsApp in one tap.** A ready message for confirmation, pickup reminder, return reminder and thank you, from the shop's own WhatsApp.

**Works anywhere**
- Phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install. It can be added to the phone's home screen like an app.

**Being built (never promise a date):** automatic WhatsApp messages and reminders, and a staff performance tracker. A separate mobile app comes later.

## 8. A five-minute demo

Use the demo shop on a phone. Say the short line beside each step.

1. **Open the Dashboard.** "This is your morning: what goes out today, what comes back, what is late."
2. **Find a dress.** Type its name or scan its tag. "You see how many are free in each branch."
3. **Make a booking.** Pick dates first, then the dress, then the customer. "It will not let you pick a dress that is not free."
4. **Hand it over.** Take the deposit. "The deposit is recorded and cannot be given back by mistake."
5. **Return it.** Tick each piece. Show a late charge. "The system works out the late fee. You can change it."
6. **Open Finance.** "Today's cash, what is owed, the deposits you hold, your expenses and your profit."
7. **Show a receipt.** "For a GST shop this is a tax invoice with its own number."

End with: **"Shall we start your 14-day trial with your own stock list? Please keep it ready."**

- A staff performance tracker, or a date for it.

- Languages other than English.
- A button for a shop to download all its own data.
- Any feature a shop asks for, until Zypher has said yes in writing.
- A price below the list, a different initial cost, or more than 14 trial days.
